ScholarshipAI
Global AI Scholarship Eligibility & University Matching Platform

Tagline

"Find scholarships you're most likely to win."

Problem

Millions of students waste months applying for scholarships they don't qualify for because:

Every country uses different grading systems.
Universities have different admission requirements.
Students don't know how competitive they are.
Most don't have professional SOPs or CVs.
Few understand how publications, IELTS, work experience, research, volunteering, leadership, and awards affect admission.

ScholarshipAI solves this problem using AI.

App Store & Play Store Compliance

To maximize approval chances:

Clearly state that eligibility scores and admission probabilities are estimates, not guarantees.
Use official payment systems for digital content sold within the app where required by Apple and Google.
Obtain user consent before processing academic records and personal documents.
Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest.
Provide a privacy policy, terms of service, and account deletion option.
Avoid scraping websites that prohibit automated access; instead, use official APIs, open datasets, RSS feeds, or obtain permission where required.
Long-Term Vision

ScholarshipAI evolves beyond a scholarship search tool into a global academic intelligence platform. By standardizing education systems worldwide, evaluating complete applicant profiles, and using AI to recommend the most suitable opportunities, it becomes a personalized education advisor that helps students identify the right scholarships, strengthen weak areas of their applications, and manage the entire journey from academic assessment to admission and funding. This differentiated approach creates a scalable platform with global appeal while remaining compliant with major mobile app marketplace requirements.
